📊 Retail Sales Intelligence Dashboard

An interactive Business Intelligence dashboard built in **Tableau** to analyze retail sales performance across products, regions, customers, and time.

📌 Project Overview

This project transforms raw retail transaction data into actionable business insights using Tableau.

The dashboard enables users to monitor key business metrics, identify sales trends, compare regional performance, evaluate product categories, and analyze customer purchasing behavior.

 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor Total Sales, Profit, Orders, and Average Discount
- Identify high-performing product categories
- Analyze monthly sales trends
- Compare regional sales performance
- Discover top-performing products
- Identify valuable customers
- Enable interactive business exploration using filters
